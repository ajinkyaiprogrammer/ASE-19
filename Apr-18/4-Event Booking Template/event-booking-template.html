Any Queries?

Following are some common question you can check for quick help.

How do I access premium lounge passes?

Premium lounge passes are available as an add-on during checkout for select venues. Kinetic Platinum members receive automatic access to all partner lounges globally.

What is the Kinetic Refund Policy?

Kinetic offers a full refund within 7 days of purchase for most digital services or a 90-day window for vehicle bookings. Refunds are typically processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Can I transfer my tickets to another member?

Most Kinetic tickets and session passes can be transferred to another member directly through your online account dashboard or by contacting support. Simply provide the recipient's details at least 24 hours in advance to ensure the barcode or credit is successfully reassigned.
